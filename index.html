How it works

Each letter is swapped with a fixed partner from the DFFL alphabet. Uppercase letters stay uppercase, spaces and punctuation stay exactly where they are, and decoding uses the inverse map.

a b c d e f g h i j k l m↕d f l k s j h g q w e r t
n o p q r s t u v w x y z↕z u i o p m b v c x a y

No account, no tracking, no server-side text storage. Share links keep the text in the URL fragment.
